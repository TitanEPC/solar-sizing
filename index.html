🎯Sizing Basis — Today or Future Load?
Each component can be designed independently for today's actual load or the future projected load (based on growth rate × horizon set in Step 1). Sizing for today = lower cost, more competitive quote. Sizing for future = more headroom, less likely to need upgrades.
☀️ Solar PV Array
Always kept at today's load + 8% contingency (prevents clipping). Future sizing not recommended for PV.
⚡ Inverter
Today: sizes on current peak demand (cheaper, right-sized). Future: adds growth buffer to peak — useful if load expansion is planned soon.
🔋 Battery Bank
Today: most competitive quote — size only what's needed now. Future: larger bank accommodates load growth but increases cost significantly.
